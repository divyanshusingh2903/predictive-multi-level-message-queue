Rename the project brand from PMLMQ to **Harbinger**, keeping `Predictive Multi-Level Message Queue` as the descriptor/subtitle so research lineage survives. Full rename: symbols, filenames, CMake targets, proto package, binaries, docs, CI. Single commit, then clean rebuild + full test verification.
