\section{Conclusion}
Learning each message key's duration from acknowledgements is enough to schedule shortest-expected-first with no
per-endpoint configuration, and it beats both FIFO and hand-tuned priorities on a production-style workload, including
on separate cloud hardware and against RabbitMQ and Redis. The gain depends on the regime: it needs keys that recur,
and in long backlogs it needs an aging design that does not flatten the order. Where keys do not recur, or long
jobs carry most of the work, it does not help and can hurt. Learned models remain untested against these workloads.
